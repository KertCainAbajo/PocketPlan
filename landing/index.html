FAQ

Questions, answered

Is PocketPlan really free?

Yes. There are no subscriptions, ads or in-app purchases.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. You log your own income and expenses, so there's nothing to link and no bank details to share.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

It's distributed directly as an APK. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time — that's normal for apps installed outside the Play Store.

How do I sign in without a password?

Enter your email and PocketPlan sends you a 6-digit code. Type it in and you're signed in. You can also add a PIN or fingerprint lock.
