Features

Everything you need to stay on budget

Built for students living on an allowance — simple enough to use every day.

Spending dashboard

See today's spending, your balance and where your money goes, at a glance.

Budgets that warn you

Set daily, weekly or monthly limits per category and get an alert before you overspend.

Savings goals

Save toward a new phone, a trip or an emergency fund, and watch your progress grow.

Recurring allowance

Add your weekly or monthly allowance once and PocketPlan records it for you.

PIN & fingerprint lock

Keep your finances private with a 4-digit PIN or your fingerprint.

No password needed

Sign in with a 6-digit code sent to your email. Nothing to remember.

How it works

Up and running in a minute

Sign up with your email

Enter your email, type the code we send you, and add your name.

Add your allowance

Record your income and set a budget for the week or month.

Log as you spend

Add expenses in seconds and let PocketPlan keep you on track.
