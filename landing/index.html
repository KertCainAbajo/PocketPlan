Fair questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no subscriptions, ads or in-app purchases.

Do I need to connect my bank or GCash?

No. You log your own income and expenses, so there's nothing to link and no account details to share.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

It's shared directly as an APK. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time, which is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store.

Why was the first load slow?

PocketPlan runs on free hosting, and the server sleeps when nobody is using it. The first request after a quiet spell can take up to a minute. After that it's awake.

How do I sign in without a password?

Enter your email and PocketPlan sends you a 6-digit code. Type it in and you're in. You can also add a PIN or fingerprint lock.
